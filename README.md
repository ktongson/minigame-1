# Minigame 1
## Devlog
I would describe Components, GameObjects, and Scenes like setting up Killjoy’s utility on defense. Components are like the individual parts that give her utility its purpose, such as detection range, damage, health, or the ability to activate when an enemy gets close. These different features come together to make a GameObject, like Killjoy’s Alarmbot or Turret. The Scene is like your entire defensive setup on a site, where your Turret, Alarmbot, Nanoswarms, teammates, and everything else are placed and work together. Just like changing one piece of Killjoy’s setup can affect how well the entire site is defended, changing the Components of a GameObject can affect how everything works together in the Scene.
